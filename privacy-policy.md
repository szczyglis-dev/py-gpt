## Privacy of Your Data

### Local Data and Data Handling

PyGPT is a desktop application and stores its application data and configuration locally unless a particular feature, integration, or Provider requires data to be transmitted to an external service.

The Author does not receive your conversation content, prompts, generated responses, files, or API keys merely because you use the App. Limited technical information may be transmitted when required for features such as version checks, updates, or other explicitly enabled online functionality.

### Third-Party AI Providers and External Services

PyGPT acts as a client for third-party AI models, APIs, and other external services. When you select or configure an external Provider, data necessary to perform your request may be transmitted to that Provider.

Depending on the feature being used, transmitted data may include prompts, conversation context, instructions, attachments, images, audio, files, tool inputs and outputs, or other content required to process your request.

The processing, storage, retention, logging, use, and possible use for service improvement or model training of data sent to a third-party Provider are governed by that Provider's own privacy policy, terms of service, API terms, data retention rules, and account settings.

Different Providers may apply different rules, and those rules may also differ between consumer services, commercial services, enterprise products, and APIs. Before using a Provider through PyGPT, you should review the current privacy policy, terms of service, data usage policy, and retention policy published on that Provider's official website.

This applies to services including, but not limited to: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI, Perplexity, Hugging Face, and any other third-party Provider, API, model endpoint, plugin, tool, or external service that you configure or use with PyGPT.

### API Keys and Credentials

API keys and other credentials entered into PyGPT are used to authenticate requests to the corresponding Provider or service. They are not intentionally transmitted to the Author.

Your credentials may necessarily be transmitted to the relevant Provider as part of the authentication process when making API requests. You are responsible for protecting your API keys, configuring appropriate usage limits where available, and complying with the Provider's security and account requirements.

### Plugins, Tools and Integrations

Some plugins, tools, integrations, web services, or optional App features may communicate with third-party services independently of the selected AI Provider. Data required to perform such operations may therefore be sent to those services.

You should review the privacy policies and terms of any external services that you enable or configure in the App.

### Local Models and Services

PyGPT may also be configured to work with locally hosted models or services. When using a fully local configuration, data processing may remain on your local device, depending on the model, software, plugins, tools, and integrations you choose to use.
